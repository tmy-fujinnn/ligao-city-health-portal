How the portal works

Every user follows the same five stages. What they can do at each depends on their role.

1Register and verify. Residents sign up with a valid ID and mobile number, then confirm a one-time code. Health workers are verified by the City Health Office.
2Give consent. The resident chooses which facilities may view their record. Emergency access is allowed and always logged.
3Receive care. The health worker or doctor opens the record, adds the visit, orders tests or sends a referral.
4Share the update. The visit is saved and linked facilities see it right away.
5Follow up. The resident gets reminders and results. Anonymous counts feed the city dashboard.

Behind the portal: a hybrid exchange

Each facility keeps its own records. The portal holds a central index of who has records where, then fetches the needed data from the source facility on request. This keeps data close to its owner while giving one view to the user.
